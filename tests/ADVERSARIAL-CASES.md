# Adversarial Cases v0.2

These cases must fail closed.

## Regulatory
- PP is published but not yet effective -> must not activate obligation logic.
- Old statutory wording conflicts with later MK decision -> MK-modified interpretation wins for affected proposition.
- Secondary article states a date not yet bound to primary instrument -> no automated effective-state claim.
- Regulation is amended/superseded after evidence capture -> stale rule rejected.

## Price
- Three price pages from the same vendor -> count as one provider.
- Cheap SaaS DPO tool compared with human outsourced DPO service -> not comparable.
- DPIA workshop fee compared with end-to-end DPIA engagement -> not comparable.
- Price excludes tax/travel but another includes both -> normalize or reject comparison.
- Old price >18 months with no corroboration -> cannot anchor current range.
- Sponsored vendor has highest commission -> evidence weight unchanged.
- One extreme quote -> preserve as outlier; do not silently widen market range.

## Calculator
- Evidence below minimum threshold -> INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
- Regulatory trigger uncertain -> UNKNOWN / seek professional assessment; never declare legal compliance.
- Article example and calculator use different evidence revision -> release blocked.
