# PDP Cost Driver Taxonomy v0.4

Purpose: explain why quotes differ without inventing market prices.

## DPO / privacy operating model
- service model: internal | fractional/external | managed team
- organization scale
- business units / legal entities
- jurisdictions
- data-subject scale
- special/sensitive data exposure
- processing complexity
- vendor/processor count
- incident-response availability
- meeting/reporting cadence
- policy/document workload
- data-subject request workload
- training scope
- audit/regulator support
- on-site requirement
- contract term

## DPIA
- number of processing activities
- system/process complexity
- high-risk characteristics
- data-flow discovery maturity
- stakeholder count
- workshop/interview count
- technical architecture review
- threat/risk analysis depth
- remediation design
- final report/review rounds
- post-remediation reassessment

## Gap assessment / privacy audit
- entity/site count
- policy/document inventory
- process inventory
- system inventory
- sample/testing depth
- interviews
- technical-control review
- evidence collection
- remediation roadmap
- executive presentation
- retest/follow-up

Rule: cost drivers describe scope. They are not prices and cannot be converted to money without eligible evidence.
