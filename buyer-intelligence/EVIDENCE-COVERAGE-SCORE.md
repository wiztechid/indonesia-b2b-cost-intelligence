# Evidence Coverage Score v0.4

This is a data-completeness score, NOT a vendor-quality score and NOT legal-compliance scoring.

## Dimensions (100 total)
- source identity & ownership: 10
- observation/quote date: 10
- price/commercial terms: 15
- service definition: 15
- scope quantities: 15
- deliverables/exclusions: 15
- tax/add-on clarity: 10
- contract term/renewal: 10

## States
- 85–100: HIGH coverage
- 65–84: MEDIUM coverage
- <65: LOW coverage

## Hard blockers
Regardless of score, a record cannot support market-range calculation when:
- source/provider identity is unknown;
- service type is ambiguous;
- amount is missing;
- scope is fundamentally non-comparable;
- evidence is discovery-only.

## Important
Coverage measures how complete the evidence record is. It does not imply that a vendor is better, cheaper, compliant, certified, or suitable.
