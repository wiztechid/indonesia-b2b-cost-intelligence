# Comparability Rules v0.5

## Hard non-comparable
- different service type
- training/certification vs professional service
- project vs recurring managed service when components cannot be separated
- material deliverables cannot be mapped
- foreign-currency normalization lacks rate source/date
- quote identity or evidence revision is missing

## Partial comparability
Use only when a meaningful common scope exists but material extras, exclusions, limits, SLA, or term differ.

## Comparable
Requires the same service type, materially equivalent common base deliverables, normalizable commercial term, explicit treatment of taxes/add-ons, and no hidden material quantity-limit mismatch.

## Cost presentation
Never show cheapest-to-most-expensive ranking unless the compared cost basis is identical. Prefer comparable base cost, additional included scope, excluded/unknown cost, and contract commitment.

## Pairwise principle
Comparability is pairwise. A may be comparable with B while A is non-comparable with C. Never force a group-level winner or average.
