# Indonesia B2B Cost Intelligence

Independent buyer-side cost and procurement intelligence for Indonesian businesses.

## Beachhead
Privacy/PDP compliance and security procurement cost intelligence.

## Launch discipline
No generic "best software" listicles. Initial publication is limited to six PDP buyer-intent pages plus one PDP Compliance Cost Calculator after evidence and regulatory gates pass.

## Core principles
- buyer-side neutrality
- evidence-backed ranges, never invented market prices
- advertised price, quotation, estimate, and calculated TCO are distinct evidence classes
- regulation publication/issuance and effective dates are distinct states
- one search intent has one canonical owner
- article/calculator parity
- legal/compliance content is educational budgeting intelligence, not legal advice
- ads, affiliates, sponsorships, and leads never change methodology
