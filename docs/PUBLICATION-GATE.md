# Publication Gate v1.1

Every public page must PASS all gates.

## 1 Exact Intent
Answer the buyer question directly. Cost pages surface an evidence-supported range or explicitly state insufficient evidence.

## 2 Independent Evidence
No "market price" from one vendor. Preserve source class and observation date.

## 3 Freshness
Every price/regulatory evidence item has checked_at and, when applicable, published_at/effective_at.

## 4 Range Integrity
Keep distinct:
- advertised_price
- vendor_quote
- third_party_estimate
- calculated_tco
- regulatory_fee

Never silently blend classes.

## 5 Commercial Neutrality
Affiliate, sponsor, or lead relationship cannot alter methodology, inclusion, score, or range.

## 6 Calculator Parity
Article examples and calculator outputs use the same versioned assumptions.

## 7 Cannibalization
One primary intent = one canonical owner. Supporting pages must not retarget the same query.

## 8 Legal Boundary
Educational budgeting intelligence only; not legal advice. Legal obligation claims require authoritative evidence.

## 9 Ad/Lead Separation
Advertising and lead generation remain outside editorial/evidence logic.

## 10 Regulatory-State Gate
Fail closed unless each legal proposition records:
- jurisdiction
- instrument
- provision/decision
- source authority
- issued_or_published_at
- effective_at when applicable
- status: proposed | issued_not_effective | effective | amended | superseded | judicially_modified
- checked_at

Never infer "effective now" merely because an instrument has been issued or published. Judicial modifications must be represented independently from the original statutory text.

## Fail-closed rule
If price evidence, regulatory state, calculator assumptions, or intent ownership is ambiguous, do not publish the affected claim/output.
